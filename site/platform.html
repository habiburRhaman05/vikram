01 - Reception

A dedicated line, and someone always on it

Your practice gets its own local number for calls and texts. Behind it sits an AI receptionist that knows your services, your hours and your calendar - so the caller who reaches you at 9pm gets a booking, not a beep.

  • Your own number - keep your personal phone out of it, or port your existing line
  • Answers in English or Spanish, matching how the caller speaks
  • Qualifies the caller, books the slot and texts the confirmation
  • Escalates anything it shouldn't handle, with the call summary attached

02 - Conversations

One thread per client, whatever channel they use

Clients don't respect channel boundaries. They call, then text, then reply to an old email. Your team should still see one continuous history.

Every channel merged

Calls, SMS, email, website chat, WhatsApp and social messages arrive in the same place, attached to the same client record.

Shared across the team

Anyone can pick up a conversation without asking who spoke to the client last, or digging through a personal inbox.

Website chat included

A chat widget on your site answers common questions instantly and captures the visitor as a lead before they leave.

03 - Client documents

Documents that collect themselves

Every client gets a checklist built for their return type. They upload from their phone, files land on their record, and the follow-up runs whether or not anyone remembers to send it.

  • Checklists per return type - 1040, Schedule C, 1065, 1120, 1120S, 1041 and more
  • Camera uploads, so nobody needs a scanner or a desktop
  • Automatic reminders for what's still outstanding, by text and email
  • A "what's missing" view across every open return, not just one client

04 - Workflow

The whole season on one board

Each client sits in exactly one stage. Moving them fires the right message automatically - so status updates stop being a task someone has to remember.

New lead Contacted Docs requested Docs received In preparation Review Ready to file Paid

Automations at each stage

New-lead replies, appointment reminders, missing-document chasers and no-show recovery all fire from the stage the client is in.

Booking that fills itself

Clients book online or through the receptionist. Confirmations and reminders go out automatically, cutting no-shows.

Capacity you can see

Returns per preparer, time in each stage, and where the season is backing up - visible before it becomes a problem.

05 - Signing, billing & reputation

Close the loop without leaving the system

Unlimited e-signature

Engagement letters, authorisation forms and consents sent for signature and returned to the client record - with no per-signature charge.

Invoicing & card payments

Send the prep fee as an invoice, take payment online, and see what is outstanding without exporting anything to a spreadsheet.

Review requests, automatically

Once a return is filed and paid, the system asks for a review at the moment the client is happiest - the single most reliable source of new local clients.

Reporting that answers real questions

Where leads came from, how many converted, how long returns sit in each stage, and which preparer is carrying the season.

Website & lead capture

A fast, mobile-first site with your branding, enquiry forms that create a client record instantly, and the chat widget already wired in.
