Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect information you share with us.

Last updated: 2026

Who we are

QuickTaxnBooks operates this website and the client communication platform described on it. Our office is at 292 Washington Ave Ext, Ste 110, Albany, NY 12203. You can reach us at TaxPrep@QuickTaxnBooks.Com or (518) 250-9662.

What we collect

  • Details you submit - name, business name, email, phone number and anything you write in our contact or booking forms.
  • Communications - calls, texts, emails and chat messages exchanged with us, including recordings or transcripts where the law permits and you have been notified.
  • Usage data - standard technical information such as browser type, device and pages visited, collected through cookies and similar technologies.

How we use it

We use your information to respond to your enquiry, schedule and conduct demonstrations, provide and support the services you sign up for, and meet our legal and record-keeping obligations. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

Service providers

We use third-party providers to deliver the service - including communications infrastructure, scheduling, form handling and payment processing. They may process your information only to provide services to us and are bound by contract to protect it.

Security and retention

We apply administrative, technical and physical safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the information we handle, and we keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described here or as required by law. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, and you can opt out of marketing messages at any time by replying STOP to a text, using the unsubscribe link in an email, or contacting us directly. Some information may be retained where we are legally required to keep it.

Changes

If we update this policy, we will revise the date above and post the new version on this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your information? Email TaxPrep@QuickTaxnBooks.Com or call (518) 250-9662.
